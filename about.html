About Evervow

Your True Story

We believe that the ceremony is the heart of a wedding. It's the moment when promises are made and a new family is born. Our mission is to make that moment profoundly meaningful.

How Evervow Began

Years ago, after attending dozens of weddings where the ceremonies felt like generic placeholders before the reception, our founder realized something was missing. Couples were spending months curating their flowers, food, and music, but leaving the actual vows to a standard script downloaded from the internet.

Evervow was born out of a passion for storytelling and the belief that every couple deserves words spoken over them that resonate deeply.

Today, we are a collective of professional writers, public speakers, and ordained officiants who have had the privilege of marrying over 500 couples across the country.
